# DarkOrbit map background references

Research date: 2026-10-12. This note supports original Dorbit concept images. It does not approve a gameplay change or alter the four-map network in [GAME_PLAN.md](../../GAME_PLAN.md).

## What the official images show

The [Bigpoint DarkOrbit gallery](https://games.bigpoint.net/game/darkorbit) provides six screenshots. The following are visual observations of publisher-hosted images, not statements about every DarkOrbit map.

- [Gallery screenshot 02](https://portal-assets.bigpoint.com/game/darkorbit/screenshot/darkorbit_screenshot_500x250_02.jpg) combines a large ochre planet, orange and olive cloud bands, and steel structures along the edges. A recognizable planet gives the scene a stronger identity than a starfield alone.
- [Gallery screenshot 04](https://portal-assets.bigpoint.com/game/darkorbit/screenshot/darkorbit_screenshot_500x250_04.jpg) combines gold and green nebula light with a dark planet, an asteroid belt and an industrial station. Natural and constructed scenery share the view.
- [Official full-size screenshot 02](https://darkorbit-22.bpsecure.com/do_img/global/externalHome/assets/screenshots/screen_2.jpg) has a large crescent planet in the upper left, a smaller backlit planet in the upper right, textured orange and olive clouds, and diagonal station structures at different apparent scales. Ships and bright yellow lasers remain distinct against the clouds.
- [Official full-size screenshot 03](https://darkorbit-22.bpsecure.com/do_img/global/externalHome/assets/screenshots/screen_3.jpg) uses orange and purple nebula clouds behind a large irregular industrial structure. Bright blue laser lines separate combat from the warm background.
- [Official full-size screenshot 04](https://darkorbit-22.bpsecure.com/do_img/global/externalHome/assets/screenshots/screen_4.jpg) has a huge cropped gray planet, an arc of asteroids with constructed settlements, and scattered wreckage. This is a dense location with a specific silhouette, rather than uniformly scattered rocks.

These images suggest that Dorbit should gain a distinct distant landmark and a composed distribution of scenery on each map. That is an art-direction inference from the images, not a published DarkOrbit design rule.

## What the DarkOrbit team says

The team's [2015 graphics-engine announcement](https://board-en.darkorbit.com/threads/news-on-the-new-3d-graphics-engine-update-public-testing-tuesday-16-june-2015.102258/) says the update introduced new art for important assets while preserving how the game plays. The official screenshots use an elevated view. Dorbit's agreed full 3D flight and camera behind the ship require original compositions that remain useful when the pilot turns.

The staff-authored [Voyagers' Ascent FAQ](https://board-en.darkorbit.com/threads/faq-voyagers-ascent.130327/) describes a deep-blue nebula, a fractured ringed planet, dense asteroid fields and floating ruins. This is evidence for one recent event map, not the appearance of the older standard sectors. Its mix of celestial scenery and ruins provides another official reference for environmental variety.

## Proposed Dorbit concept directions

These are original proposals for the existing four maps. They are not reproductions of DarkOrbit maps or implementation requirements.

| Existing map | Palette and distant landmark | Scenery composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 / Outpost 01 | Cool blue and teal, a pale inhabited planet with a lit crescent | The working outpost anchors one side. Distant traffic and sparse industrial satellites surround it. Keep the station approach open. |
| M2 / Outer Patrol | Amber and muted gold, a large desert planet crescent | A long asteroid band and small patrol structures establish scale. Leave wide gaps between scenery groups. |
| M3 / Debris Fields | Rust orange and deep red, a damaged moon or planet behind dust | Broken freighter sections, station fragments and irregular rock clusters create a recognizable wreck field. Arrange major pieces around open routes. |
| M4 / Frontier | Violet and cyan, a remote planetary silhouette against a luminous nebula | Distant unfamiliar ruins and fractured rings create a different silhouette. Put detail at the sides and retain dark space behind the main flight direction. |

For all four concepts, show a small ship from behind to communicate Dorbit's camera and scale. Separate a distant celestial background from closer rocks, wrecks and structures. The background can supply richness without placing solid obstacles everywhere. Preserve darker areas so enemies, targeting markers and weapon effects remain readable.

The eventual 3D implementation would need views in several directions and measured performance with the project's Compatibility renderer. Generated single-view paintings are orientation references. They are not ready-made seamless skyboxes, collision geometry or a verified runtime scene.

## Research limits

No live account or game client was used. The study covers public publisher screenshots and staff posts, not a complete inspection of the current map roster. The historical official-renders thread returned 404 during research, so it does not support any findings here. No third-party extracted asset repository was used as a source. Source images remain external references; the concept images should use original scenery.
