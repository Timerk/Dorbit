# Reclamation campaign in the map backgrounds

The second concept pass follows the story in [PR #76](https://github.com/Timerk/Dorbit/pull/76): former human sectors fell during an alien invasion, and returning pilots establish a foothold, restore infrastructure and prepare advances into occupied territory. Outpost 01 arrives as the new forward base. The images show a proposed post-arrival M1 and occupied outer sectors, rather than a particular finalized chapter or unlock order.

The user's art direction adds visible former inhabitants, battle damage and alien resource extraction. Mining machinery, surface scars and specific visual landmarks are proposals for environmental storytelling. They do not add mining mechanics, settlement simulation or campaign objectives to GAME_PLAN.md.

| Map | Evidence of former human territory | Current situation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 / Outpost 01 | Settlement grids interrupted by bombardment craters, a shattered orbital defense ring and wrecks | The returning human forward base has working docks and construction scaffolding. |
| M2 / Outer Patrol | Abandoned surface settlements, damaged patrol relay and lost freighter remains | Alien orbital collectors strip resources from the planet. |
| M3 / Debris Fields | Broken carrier, captured salvage docks and damaged receivers | Alien processing machinery consumes the battlefield's wreckage and extracts minerals from the scarred moon. |
| M4 / Frontier | A ruined human orbital ring and dark settlement grids | A larger alien extraction complex dominates the planet, with a relay supporting the occupation. |

Human structures use angular metal frames, rectangular modules and warm service lights. The alien machines share curved segmented claws, overlapping black ceramic plates and narrow teal emissive seams. Consistent forms connect the outer maps to the same occupying force. Each sector retains its original palette and an open central flight area.

These pictures can suggest future reconnaissance, recovery and restoration locations. Exact mission use remains campaign design work. Their visual direction is now implemented as client-only 3D backdrops; see the [runtime review](../../docs/sector-art/campaign-backgrounds.md). The pictures remain single-view references rather than seamless skyboxes. Both concept passes are preserved for comparison.
