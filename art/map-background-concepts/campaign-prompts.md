## M1

Use case: precise-object-edit / stylized-concept. Edit this Dorbit M1 environment concept into a campaign-aware v2, one wide landscape picture. Preserve its beautiful blue/cyan space palette, enormous habitable planet upper left, behind-ship perspective, small player ship lower center, working human outpost at right, and open dark central flight corridor. Narrative: this was a thriving human colony conquered during an alien invasion. Humans are now returning with Outpost 01 as a forward reclamation base. THE PLANET MUST VISIBLY TELL THIS STORY at first glance: show recognizable connected networks of old cities and planned settlement grids on the continent/night edge, with large dark dead districts and a few surviving dim lights; several broad blackened circular bombardment scars cutting through those settlement patterns; scorched streaks and collapsed sprawling city footprints visible from orbital distance, and a few thin ash plumes rooted on the surface. Retain oceans, clouds, blue atmosphere and natural terrain. This is a once-inhabited damaged world, not a molten lava planet or totally destroyed globe. Near the planet limb, add a shattered old orbital defense ring: torn metal arcs, burned defense platforms and several recognizable derelict human warship sections, mostly off center. At the right, show the NEW human forward outpost operational but still being built, brighter clean repair panels, a small scaffolding wing and sparse supply transports, contrasted with older ruined orbital structures. The old war is over here; show aftermath and early reconstruction, not an active battle or spectacular ongoing explosions. Rich detailed grounded sci-fi game art, dramatic restrained lighting, clear silhouettes, immense scale. Keep enemies/gameplay legible in central space. No text, HUD, labels, logos, watermark, dense debris wall across route, or giant foreground player ship.
